How a question is built

One pair, two dimensions

Each question contrasts two indicators from different dimensions — a cross-dimension pairing. That is deliberate: it forces a real preference trade-off instead of asking the same dimension twice.

The framework

Eight dimensions, thirty-two indicators

From the Secondary Indicators Framework V1.0. Open a dimension to see its four observable indicators — the visual evidence coders look for in every image.

MMinimalism▾

A preference for simplified visual language, clear structure, restrained decoration and ordered space.

M01 · Form SimplificationClean silhouettes, reduced elements, geometric expression
M02 · Visual SpaceNegative space, breathing room between elements
M03 · Decorative RestraintLow ornament, simplified detail, no visual stacking
M04 · Order & ProportionStructural balance, visual order, harmonious ratios
HHeritage▾

A preference for historical continuity, traditional forms and classic cultural symbols.

H01 · Historical ContinuityHistoric styles carried forward, classic design language
H02 · Traditional SymbolsTraditional patterns, cultural symbols, historic ornament
H03 · Classic FormsTimeless silhouettes, stable proportions
H04 · Cultural HeritageRegional culture, traditional craft, cultural identity
RRomanticism▾

A preference for softness, delicacy, emotional atmosphere and poetic expression.

R01 · Soft LinesFlowing curves, gentle contours, nothing rigid
R02 · Lightness & FlowLight materials, drift and movement
R03 · Poetic NarrativeMood, natural imagery, a sense of story
R04 · Delicate DetailsFine detail, soft ornament, delicate texture
SSensuality▾

A preference for body awareness, sensory experience, visual tension and confident expression.

S01 · Body AwarenessBody contour, garments that follow or emphasise structure
S02 · Tactile MaterialsLeather, silk, sheen and touch in contrast
S03 · Visual TensionStrong contrast, conflicting lines, visual impact
S04 · Confident ExpressionBold silhouette, clear attitude, presence
AAvant-garde▾

A preference for experimentation, unconventional structure, conceptual expression and breaking convention.

A01 · Structural ExperimentationDeconstruction, unconventional construction and combination
A02 · Unconventional ProportionExaggerated proportion, asymmetry, shifted forms
A03 · Conceptual ExpressionAbstract ideas, art concepts, non-narrative expression
A04 · Material InnovationUnusual materials, recombination, experimental surfaces
EExtravagance▾

A preference for visual richness, layered decoration, vivid colour and dramatic effect.

E01 · Decorative AbundanceRich ornament, multi-layered detail
E02 · Visual DramaA commanding focal point, theatrical effect
E03 · Colour & Pattern IntensitySaturated colour, large-scale pattern, bold combinations
E04 · Luxurious PresentationRich materials, lavish surfaces, display and polish
CCraftsmanship▾

A preference for material quality, making detail, precise structure and evident craft.

C01 · Material QualityMaterial character and how materials work together
C02 · Craft DetailsStitching, hand finishing, surface work
C03 · Making TechniquesWeaving, embroidery, carving and other techniques
C04 · Structural PrecisionExact cutting, structural integrity, finish
VVintage & Cultural Expression▾

A preference for era styles, cultural symbols, vintage visual language and cross-cultural expression.

V01 · Historical ReferencesElements quoted from other eras, period atmosphere
V02 · Cultural SymbolsRegional signs, folk elements, cultural pattern
V03 · Vintage AestheticsAged texture, retro colour, historic media feel
V04 · Cultural FusionCultures combined, tradition meeting modernity

Boundaries the coders watch. Minimalism is not Craftsmanship — visual simplicity says nothing about quality of making. Heritage is not Vintage — continuity of tradition is not a retro look. Romanticism is not Sensuality, and Extravagance is not Avant-garde: visual excess is not experimentation. These distinctions come straight from the framework, and every coded image has to respect them.

What a finished result will add

AI interpretation

A written reading of your scored profile — patterns across dimensions, tensions worth noticing, questions to explore. The AI interprets scores; it never creates them.

Evidence trail

Every score traces back to the questions, indicator codes and scoring-rule version that produced it, so a result can always be re-derived and audited.

Deep report

A longer paid report and a personal exploration archive are planned. All pricing is a hypothesis until it is validated with real users.

Results describe preferences, not fixed identity. Preferences change, and a later result is allowed to disagree with an earlier one.

About

Know yourself, a little more clearly

VIVINEA exists to help people discover what they love, understand what fits them, recognise their own patterns, and explore who they could become — through structured assessments, transparent scoring and AI interpretation, once they are live.

VIVINEA connects with the podcast BEFORE BECOMING, continuing to explore how we come to know ourselves, understand the world, and gradually become who we want to be.

It is deliberately not a psychological test, not an MBTI-style typing tool and not a diagnosis. It is a structured mirror for self-exploration and entertainment, built to be honest about what it can and cannot claim.

Question numbering

How the IDs are decided

Two numbering layers, because a question has two jobs: it appears in a sequence for the person answering, and it lives permanently in a database with a scoring record.

LayerFormatExampleRules
Display IDAES-Q + 001–016AES-Q001The answering order. Shown as “Question 1 of 16” — never as a percentage.
Internal ID[Matrix]_[Dimension]_[Seq]AES_MIN_001Matrix codes: AES, PER, LIF, PET, FUT. Dimension codes: MIN, HER, ROM, SEN, AVA, EXT, CRA, VIN for Aesthetic. The same scheme is reserved for later matrices, e.g. PER_SOC_001.
Indicator pair[Letter][01–04] × [Letter][01–04]M01 × E01Stored on every Aesthetic record alongside the internal ID, with the two image IDs, because one question always spans two dimensions.

Stability rules

  • An ID never changes once published, and is never reused.
  • Editing a question raises its version (e.g. 1.0 → 1.1); a substantial change creates a new ID.
  • Old results stay interpretable through the question, scoring-rule and content versions they were produced under.
  • IDs are identical across all language versions.

Lifecycle & open items

  • Every question moves through DRAFT → UNDER_REVIEW → PILOT → VALIDATED → PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED.
  • A question without a validated scoring rule never enters the live assessment.
  • Confirmed pairings: Q001 = M01 × E01, Q002 = M02 × E02, Q003 = M03 × E03, Q004 = M04 × E04, Q005 = H01 × V01. Pairings for Q006–Q016 are to be determined. Personality display IDs are reserved from PER-Q001 onwards. The total question count remains to be determined. Personality Identity is deferred.
